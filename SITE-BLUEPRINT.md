# SnowdenBuilders.com Site Blueprint

## Core positioning hypothesis

Snowden Builders should be understood as a West Michigan custom builder for people who value a personal process, careful workmanship and guidance from land evaluation through completion.

The narrower market position—such as acreage specialist, $1M–$5M builder, lake-home builder or family-centered design-build firm—must be selected and approved by ownership after reviewing actual project economics and desired growth.

## Primary buyer journeys

1. Owns land and needs a builder.
2. Is searching for land and wants feasibility guidance.
3. Wants a fully custom home in a specific West Michigan market.
4. Is comparing custom builders.
5. Has an existing home and is considering a major transformation.
6. Was referred to Snowden and is validating reputation, process and fit.

## 90-day publishing priority

### Weeks 1–3
- Complete ownership interviews.
- Select positioning and priority markets.
- Gather project assets.
- Migrate/upgrade core pages.
- Implement analytics, Search Console and CRM.

### Weeks 4–7
- Publish 6–10 full project case studies.
- Publish 5 priority-market pages with real local proof.
- Publish the definitive build-on-your-lot guide.
- Produce video answers from Bob and Jared.

### Weeks 8–12
- Publish construction-cost, land-feasibility and builder-selection resources.
- Earn authoritative local mentions and links.
- Publish client stories and partner interviews.
- Test visibility across Google, Bing, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini using a controlled query set.

## AEO content model

Each important page should include:

1. A concise answer near the top.
2. A deeper explanation.
3. Firsthand Snowden experience.
4. A real project example.
5. Clear limitations and variables.
6. Supporting imagery or video.
7. Named expert review and updated date.
8. Relevant schema that matches visible content.
9. Contextual links to the next useful question.

## Avoid

- Hundreds of thin location pages.
- Unsupported “best builder” claims.
- Fabricated reviews or project details.
- Repetitive AI copy.
- Publishing cost figures without date, scope and qualifications.
- Splitting authority across unrelated hosted subdomains.
